# E-Commerce Data Analysis using Pandas & NumPy

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an e-commerce dataset using Python, Pandas, and NumPy to identify meaningful patterns in products, customers, suppliers, pricing, ratings, revenue, and profitability.

The objective was to apply data analysis techniques to real-world business problems and generate data-backed insights and recommendations.

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

### Product Analysis

- Analyzed product prices and costs
- Compared product ratings with prices
- Analyzed product profitability
- Compared categories and brands

### Customer Analysis

- Identified high-value customers
- Analyzed customer revenue and profit
- Compared customers based on order frequency
- Evaluated customer contribution to overall business performance

### Supplier Analysis

- Calculated average potential profit margin by supplier
- Compared supplier performance
- Identified suppliers offering stronger profit margins

### Profitability Analysis

- Identified loss-making orders
- Analyzed factors contributing to negative profit
- Examined relationships between selling price, cost, and profit margin

### Business Analysis

- Combined findings from pricing, cost, margin, ratings, categories, brands, customers, and suppliers
- Identified major business trends
- Developed data-backed business recommendations

## Key Business Insights

1. Supplier performance was compared using average potential profit margin to identify suppliers with stronger margin opportunities.

2. Product ratings and prices were analyzed to determine whether higher-rated products tend to have higher prices.

3. Customer and order-level profitability was analyzed to identify high-value customers and understand the major factors behind loss-making orders.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize suppliers with consistently stronger profit margins.
- Review pricing and cost structures for loss-making products and orders.
- Focus customer retention strategies on high-value customers.
- Use product rating and pricing patterns to improve product positioning and pricing decisions.
- Continuously monitor supplier, product, and customer profitability.
